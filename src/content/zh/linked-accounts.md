# 关联账号 {#linked-accounts}

把外部账号连到 Daytona。

Daytona supports linking user accounts from 身份提供商s.

- Google
- GitHub

## Link account {#link-account}

1. Go to [Daytona Dashboard ↗](https://app.daytona.io/dashboard/user/account-settings) account settings
2. Click Link Account next to the provider you want to link
3. Follow the prompts to link your account

## Unlink account {#unlink-account}

1. Go to [Daytona Dashboard ↗](https://app.daytona.io/dashboard/user/account-settings) account settings
2. Click Unlink next to the provider you want to unlink
3. Follow the prompts to unlink your account
